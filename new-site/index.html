What we do

One crew. Every kind of roof.

Homes, condo associations, builders and commercial buildings, all handled by the same in-house team.

Residential

We warehouse shingles in bulk for true wholesale pricing, bend all flashing and decorative metal in our own shop, and send only long-tenured Hamstra employees to your home.

Learn more

Multi-Family

Condo boards and property managers get unlimited-license credentials, crews that stage work to minimize resident disruption, and documentation of every step for HOA records.

Learn more

New Construction

Builders get 40 years of production know-how, elite GAF and Owens Corning certifications, and a sheet-metal shop that fabricates custom vents, chimney caps and accent panels.
